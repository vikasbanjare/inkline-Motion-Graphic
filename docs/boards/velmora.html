Two journeys, one cup

A 36-second launch film for Velmora's first café. A coffee cherry and a man on his way to work cross a city at dawn, and meet in one cup.

Logline

A man walking to work and a coffee bean travelling from the hills cross the city before sunrise, and meet in one cup at Velmora's new café.

Device

Parallel journeys: intercut bean ↔ man (A / B / A / B), shots shortening as they converge, then one slow payoff.

Motif

Circles and steam: the drop, the cherry, the roaster drum, the tray, the cup. Steam from a street vent rhymes with steam from the cup.

Protagonist

A man in a fedora and an ochre coat. The coat never changes colour, so we always find him. He always walks left to right.

Arc

Set-up: a blue, sleepy city. Turn: the café's sign lights and the door opens on warm light. Payoff: the first sip, as the sun reaches the street.

Light

Night blue → dawn rose → morning gold. Time visibly passes; the last shot is the warmest.

Pace curve

Shots shorten from 3.5 s to 2.2 s as the two journeys converge. A beat of near-silence follows the door, then the 5-second payoff.

The board

What gets built

  • 2D the coffee drop and its bloom (opener)
  • lathe + blob layered hills in mist, rows of shrubs, the coffee branch and cherries
  • Person the man: steps out of a door, walks, turns a corner, enters, lifts a cup
  • extrude + box a blue-hour street: doorways, shopfronts, lamps that switch off one by one, a steam vent
  • lathe + tube the drum roaster, the cooling tray, the grinder, the gooseneck kettle and dripper
  • 2D foil the rosetta, the café sign lighting up, the end card

Sound plan

  • Music: lo-fi jazz, about 84 BPM: Rhodes 9th chords, upright bass, brushed kit, vinyl air. It enters soft on the hook, adds brushes when he starts walking, and drops to one Rhodes chord under the door.
  • Foley: the drop's tick, footsteps on his steps, a lamp's click as each light goes out, the drum's hum and bean rattle, the grinder's crunch, the pour, the café door bell, the cup on the saucer.
  • Silence: a short near-silence after the bell, then the payoff chord.
  • No generic whooshes or beeps on cuts. Joins are carried by foley and the music's phrasing.
