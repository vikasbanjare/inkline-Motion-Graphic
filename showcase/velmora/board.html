Two journeys, one cup

Fictional brand and claims: a sample film to show the kit. Not a real company. · 36.7 s · 12 shots

logline

A man walking to work and a coffee bean travelling from the hills cross the city before sunrise, and meet in one cup at Velmora's new café.

device

Parallel journeys: bean / man intercut, shots shortening as they converge, then one slow payoff.

motif

Circles and steam: the drop, the cherry, the drum, the tray, the lamps, the cup.

protagonist

A man in an ochre coat and a dark fedora, always walking left to right.

arc

A blue, sleepy city → the café's door opens on warm light → the first cup as the sun reaches the street.
